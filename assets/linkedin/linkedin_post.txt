A Microsoft Sentinel Operations handbook. Built for the platform team behind the query. Free. Public.

A KQL query isn't a production detection. Everything between "it runs in the query editor" and "the SOC can rely on it, monitor it, and afford it" is what this book covers.

From workspace architecture and the Azure-portal/Defender-portal split → data ingestion and table tiers → analytics-rule types → entity mapping and the incident model → detection-content lifecycle → automation rules and playbooks → UEBA and AI-assisted operations → Defender XDR signal convergence → cost and retention governance → tuning and troubleshooting.

Inside: Log Analytics workspace design, the Azure-portal-to-Defender-portal migration (and its published March 2027 retirement date), Data Collection Rules, Analytics vs. Data lake tiers, Scheduled/NRT/Fusion/Anomaly/ML Behavior Analytics/Custom Detection rules, Content Hub and detection-as-code, workbooks, hunting, watchlists, UEBA, Security Copilot and agentic tooling, automation rules, Logic Apps playbooks, commitment-tier billing mechanics, and Sentinel-specific health monitoring.

This one is honest about being a single-vendor platform-operations book, not a detection-engineering curriculum — it's a companion volume to The Detection Engineering Handbook, picking up exactly where that book's KQL chapter leaves off. And there's no lab tenant behind it: every platform fact carries a dated citation back to one of 43 sources — Microsoft Learn documentation, plus MITRE ATT&CK and NIST where the concept is vendor-neutral — or is labeled a conceptual sketch. Never a captured screenshot, never a validated-in-production claim presented as one.
